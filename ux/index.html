01 · Kiko

The World Cup,
in your hand.

01 · Kiko

The World Cup, in your pocket.

Companion app for the 2026 World Cup: live matches, tickets in Wallet and an assistant featuring the mascot Zayu.

My role
I led the user interface design.
Focus
Clear layouts, translucent surfaces and graphics optimized for a global audience.

Kiko · Users

Who are we designing for?

What time does Mexico play?🧑
Casual fan
Pain point

Match info is scattered across several apps.

User needs

See schedules and live scores at a glance.

Where did I put my ticket?👩
Stadium-goer
Pain point

Arrives without knowing the weather, time or entrance.

User needs

Ticket in Wallet and reminders to arrive on time.

I'm in Mexico… now what?🧳
International tourist
Pain point

Doesn't know the city or how to get around.

User needs

A friendly assistant to guide them through the tournament.

Every screen, one clear task.

  • Live matches
  • Match details
  • Ticket in Wallet
  • Zayu assistant always available

An app to discover cultural spots in Guadalajara (museums, parks and galleries): ticket purchase and public-transit route in a single flow.

My role
Lead of the design and development teams.
Outcome
I split the team into focused groups to speed up delivery and balanced engineering with visual design.

Tixal · Users

Who are we designing for?

How do I get to the Cabañas?🧳
Tourist in Guadalajara
Pain point

Doesn't know the public-transit routes.

User needs

Clear routes with estimated arrival time.

What should I do this weekend?🧑
Young local
Pain point

Doesn't know which museums there are or what they cost.

User needs

Discover places and buy tickets in one flow.

There are four of us, how much is it?👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
Family
Pain point

User needs

From cultural spot to your route.

  • Buy a ticket
  • Pay
  • Check the route

04 · Coppi

A breather,
right on time.

04 · Coppi · Coppel Challenge, Hack 26

A breather, when it's needed most.

An emotional well-being app for employees: mood check-ins, breathing exercises and quick access to support.

My role
UX/UI design, research and prototyping.
Focus
Soft shapes, warm colors and a single action per screen so it never overwhelms in a moment of crisis.

Coppi · Users

Who are we designing for?

Today wasn't a good day🧑‍💼
Store employee
Pain point

Heavy workload and little time to take a break.

User needs

Quick exercises to manage stress.

I don't know who to talk to👩
New employee
Pain point

Doesn't know the company's support services.

User needs

Direct access to the Mental and Emotional Well-being team.

How is my team doing?👨‍💼
Area supervisor
Pain point

Notices team burnout too late.

User needs

Understand what triggers distress to act in time.

From feeling to support, in five steps.

  • Log how you feel
  • What triggered it
  • Breathing and focus game
  • Reach out for support
