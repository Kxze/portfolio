Tetris

When the game invades reality.

Client
Kalypso
Year
2025
Location
Jalisco, Mexico
Services
Post-production · Photography · Editing · Color

A fictional short film where a person discovers a forgotten arcade machine at home, and as they play, the aesthetics and logic of Tetris begin to invade the real world. I directed the project from start to finish — from concept and planning to on-set execution — while also handling cinematography, VFX supervision and editing, crafting a progressively immersive experience driven by geometry, light and the tension of breaking an impossible high score.

02 · Stop-motion commercial

Prozan

Premium supplements to enhance your wellness, health and beauty from within.

Watch on Instagram
Client
Prozan
Year
2025
Location
Jalisco, Mexico · Remote
Services
Post-production · Color grading

Prozan, a brand focused on nutritious products with a variety of flavors, commissioned a stop-motion commercial to communicate its benefits. The project showcased the brand’s attributes through frame-by-frame animation, emphasizing texture, rhythm and product clarity. I handled green-screen cleanup and the removal of animation rigs, as well as the final color grade to unify the look, highlight the food and make the nutrients and flavors more appealing.

Green screen & rig removal · Colorist

03 · Short film

Archivo Mental

The day a thought couldn’t be filed.

Client
Personal
Year
2025
Location
Jalisco, Mexico
Services
Post-production · Color · Photography · Editing

I was invited to develop the visual concept for a short film that depicts the mind as an internal office where thoughts are received and organized. I built a cinematic approach focused on visual storytelling and the emotional progression of the narrative, working as director of photography, editor, colorist, VFX artist and 3D modeling and texturing lead — designing the look, lighting, editing rhythm and color to support the dramatic arc until a thought triggers an anxiety episode and breaks the system’s order.

DP · Editor · Colorist · VFX · 3D lead
